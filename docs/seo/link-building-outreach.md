# Link-Building Outreach Plan — woolet.co

**Goal**: lift Authority Score from 21 → 35+ in 6 months via 15–25 topically relevant editorial backlinks. Focus on eyewear, mens-fashion, and big-and-tall communities — exactly the audiences where "frames for 155 mm+ faces" is a recognised pain point.

**Cadence**: 5 outreach emails/week. Track in a simple sheet (domain, contact, sent date, status, link URL).

---

## Tier 1 — Reddit + community seeding (week 1)

These already rank on page 1 for our main keywords. Native, helpful answers — not spam.

| Subreddit | Why | Action |
|---|---|---|
| r/bigmenfashionadvice | Currently ranks #5 for "glasses for wide faces" | Reply to threads asking about 150 mm+ frames with a sizing breakdown + soft mention of Woolet 158 mm + bespoke 150–172 mm tier |
| r/glasses | Active sizing-question community | Answer "are my glasses too wide" / "what size for 150 mm face" threads with measurement guide link to `/en/blog/how-to-measure-face-width-for-glasses` |

**Rule**: never lead with the product. Lead with the measurement tutorial; mention the brand only if asked or if directly relevant.

## What NOT to do

- No paid guest-post networks (PBNs, link farms). They are why Authority Score is stuck at 21.
- No anchor-text manipulation. Current anchor distribution is healthy (brand-dominated: `woolet.co` / `woolet` 95% of links). Keep it that way.
- No reciprocal links with unrelated sites.
- No mass directory submissions.
