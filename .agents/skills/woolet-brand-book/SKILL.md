# Woolet Brand System v1.0

Single source of truth for how Woolet looks, sounds and feels.
Italian Mazzucchelli acetate, handmade in the EU, built honestly wide.
**Quiet luxury — gold is a spice, not a sauce.**

---

## 01 — Positioning

Two problems nobody else solves:

- **Frame width** — standard frames stop at 138–148 mm. Woolet signature runs **158 mm**, bespoke **145–162 mm**.
- **Bridge width** — standard 18–20 mm bridges dig into wide noses. Woolet keyhole runs **21–22 mm** signature, **20–24 mm** bespoke, matched by fit scan.

**Core brand truth:** "You are not too wide. The frame is too narrow."

- **Who:** men, 40+, considered buyers who can't find frames that fit.
- **Against:** Ray-Ban, Persol, Warby Parker, Moscot — none built wide.

---

## 02 — Logo

- Primary lockup: **mark + wordmark** (the "W" bull + Woolet).
- Ink-on-cream variant only on PDP / product surfaces.
- Clear space ≥ cap-height on all sides.
- Minimum wordmark **88 px** wide on screen.
- Never stretch, recolour outside the palette, add effects, or place the gold mark on a busy photo without a scrim.

---

## 03 — Colour

Dark canvas, lit by gold. **Gold ≤ 10% of any screen.**

| Token        | Hex       | Use                                |
| ------------ | --------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Ink Black    | `#0B0A09` | Foundation background              |
| Panel        | `#16140F` | Surfaces, cards                    |
| Gold         | `#C2A05A` | Accent (primary)                   |
| Bright Gold  | `#D8B86A` | Hover / emphasis                   |
| Cream        | `#EFE9DF` | Product backdrops, light surfaces  |
| Brick        | `#A05A3F` | "Standard / problem" states only   |
| Body Text    | `#C4BDAF` | Body copy                          |
| Muted        | `#8F897B` | Captions, secondary text           |

No gradient backgrounds. No "banner gold" full-bleed blocks.

---

## 04 — Typography

- **Display — Newsreader** (serif). Headlines, section titles, big numbers, pull-quotes. Regular & Medium only.
- **UI & Body — Archivo** (sans). Body, labels, buttons, nav, captions.

Rules:
- Emphasise **exactly one word per headline** in *italic gold*.
- Eyebrow labels: uppercase, tracking `.20em`.
- Body 16–18 px, line-height 1.6–1.7.
- Never set all-caps mid-sentence.

Scale: H1 56–84 (NR 400) · H2 40 (NR 500) · H3 26 (NR 500) · Body 16–18 (Archivo 400).

## 05 — Voice & tone

**Precise · Reassuring · Evidence-led · Understated · Numbers over adjectives.**

We say:
- "You are not too wide. The frame is too narrow."
- "158 mm — engineered for 155 mm+ faces."
- "Two bridge widths, matched to your nose by the scan."
- "Premium doesn't mean expensive. It means the right material for you."

We never say:
- "Buy now!! Only 3 left!!" — no emoji, no exclamation stacks, no fake countdowns.
- "Glasses for fat heads / big faces." — never pejorative.
- Vague luxury fluff with no number behind it.

---

## 06 — Signature element

The **two-axis fit scale**: frame width × bridge width, gold "Woolet" band vs hatched "standard" band. Reuse anywhere fit is explained.

---

## 07 — Components

- **Buttons**: one primary gold CTA per view. Hover → `#D8B86A`. **2 px radius, never pill**, never full-width "banner gold" on premium pages. Secondary = ghost / outline.
- **Eyebrow & chips**: e.g. `−40% FOUNDING`, `158 MM`. Uppercase, tracked.
- **Spec callouts**: cream panel with mm + degree values (e.g. `21 mm KEYHOLE BRIDGE`, `11° HINGE ANGLE`).

---

## 08 — Photography

- **Portraits:** men 40+ with genuinely wide faces/bridges, moody low-key lighting, dark backdrop, **always tagged with the wearer's mm**.
- **Product:** front-on, centred, on warm cream — uniform crop and padding across the catalogue.
- **No** stock smiles, **no** white seamless studio cliché, **no** over-retouching.

---

## 09 — Do & Don't

**Do**
- Lead with one honest number per claim.
- Gold ≤ 10% of the screen.
- One italic-gold word per headline.
- Show fit visually (two-axis scale).
- Generous whitespace, 1.6+ line-height.

**Don't**
- Gradient backgrounds or rounded-corner + left-accent cliché cards.
- Emoji, countdowns, fake scarcity.
- All-caps mid-sentence or stacked exclamations.
- Full-bleed "banner gold" CTA blocks.
- Mix languages within one locale.
